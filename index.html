Privacy Policy

Effective 26 July 2026 · Last updated 26 July 2026

This policy explains what data the KONGRAVE Publisher application handles and why. KONGRAVE Publisher is an internal automation tool operated by Disruptive. Its only purpose is to publish our own short-form videos ("Reels") to our own Instagram Business account (@kongrave_) through Meta's Graph API. It is not a consumer product and has no public user interface, sign-up, or login for third parties.

What the app accesses

The application uses only the credentials and identifiers of our own connected accounts, stored as encrypted secrets, strictly to publish content:

  • the Instagram Business Account ID of @kongrave_;
  • a Meta access token authorizing publication to that account;
  • the video files and captions we create and choose to publish.

The app does not collect, request, or store personal data from viewers, followers, or any other third party. It reads no one else's profile, messages, or activity.

How the data is used

The access token and account ID are used solely to create media containers and publish them to our own Instagram account via the official Meta Content Publishing API. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties. The only transmission is to Meta's own APIs (graph.facebook.com) for the sole purpose of publishing our content, as required to operate the app.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and account identifiers are held as encrypted repository secrets and are used only at publication time. Video files are stored transiently for the duration of a publish job. We retain this data only as long as the app remains in operation, and remove it when it is no longer needed.

Cookies and tracking

The application has no website or end-user interface and therefore sets no cookies and performs no tracking of any individual.

Requesting deletion

Because the app processes only our own account credentials and no third-party personal data, there is no third-party data to delete. Any question or request regarding data handling can be sent to the contact below and will be addressed promptly.
